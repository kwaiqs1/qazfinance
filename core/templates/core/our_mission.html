Our Mission

The project aims to improve the level of financial knowledge among schoolchildren and students, including children from vulnerable groups, children with disabilities, and students from low-income families.

📚

Expanding Access

Expanding access to high-quality finance education for all youth across Kazakhstan.

🌍

Supporting Inclusivity

Supporting inclusive learning for children with disabilities and low-income backgrounds.

🌟

Building Leaders

Building a community of young leaders who understand money, investing, and economic opportunity.
