Welcome to QazFinance

Kazakhstan's first teen-founded, nonprofit educational organization dedicated to financial literacy and investing.

QazFinance is the first teen-founded non-profit finance-education organization in Kazakhstan, dedicated to expanding financial literacy, investment knowledge, and economic opportunity for youth nationwide through competitions, free learning platforms, and community programs.
