Who We Are

QazFinance is Kazakhstan's first teen-founded, nonprofit educational organization dedicated to financial literacy and investing. We are committed to expanding financial knowledge and economic opportunity for youth across Kazakhstan through competitions, free learning platforms, and community programs.

Our Story

Founded by teenagers passionate about finance education, QazFinance was created to address the gap in financial literacy among young people in Kazakhstan. We recognized that many schoolchildren and students, especially those from vulnerable groups, children with disabilities, and low-income families, lacked access to quality financial education.

Our mission is to empower the next generation with the knowledge and skills they need to make informed financial decisions, understand investments, and build a brighter economic future for themselves and their communities.
